Frequently asked questions

Will this be customized for our club?

Yes. This screenshot is a sample. We load your bays, rates, hours, and branding before members see anything.

How fast can I see a demo?

You'll see a branded demo within 48 hours of booking. Typical full launch is about 2 weeks.

Is this for indoor golf in Canada?

Yes. For indoor golf and simulator bays in Canada — bay bookings, memberships, and league nights.

Do members book and pay in the app?

Members pick a bay time in the app. Checkout is yours — Stripe, Interac, or the processor you already use. No marketplace cut.

Do we need a developer to change rates?

No. The admin dashboard lets you open or close bays and change rates without a developer.
