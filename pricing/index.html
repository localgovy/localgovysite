Compared with quote-only civic platforms

Vendors in the CivicPlus class do not publish a list price. A municipal website for a small town commonly lands in the high four to five figures per year before setup, with a custom quote and annual uplifts. You find that out after a sales call.

The LocalGovy town bundle is $4,200 a year. The website alone is $1,800 a year. Those figures are on this page so a clerk can put them in a staff report without waiting on us.

That comparison is for the standard town SKUs above — not a claim about every module, every city, or a percentage discount we cannot show.
