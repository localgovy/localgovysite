Why LocalGovy exists

A custom mobile app has traditionally meant a quote in the tens of thousands, a long timeline, and a developer you have to call every time a price changes. For a café, a mosque, or a town of a few thousand people, that has never been realistic.

LocalGovy closes that gap: a branded app for retail starting at $100 a month, for faith starting at $150 a month, and for towns a mobile app at $75 a month, or the app plus one website at $250.
