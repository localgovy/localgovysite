Frequently asked questions

What businesses is this for?

Restaurants, retailers, dispensaries, salons, gyms, and services — any business with regular customers.

Do I need a developer?

No. We build it, publish it, and maintain it. Day to day, changing a price or posting a promo takes a few taps in the dashboard.

How long to launch?

You'll see a branded demo within 48 hours of booking. Typical full launch is about 2 weeks — and you pay nothing until launch.

How much does a custom retail app cost?

Plans start at $150/month for a fully branded iOS & Android app — an accessible rate compared with typical custom mobile app development. Billed annually, with one month free.

Is this just a white-label template?

No. We start from a proven, tested platform, but every app is built out with your real menu, ordering rules, and business data — not a cosmetic skin. This is what makes each app distinct, and what satisfies Apple's and Google's app store rules against duplicate, cosmetic-only apps.
