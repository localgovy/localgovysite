What residents see

News at the top of the home screen. Events, a business directory, school updates, and polls underneath. The assistant answers hours, bylaws, and pickup days so the front desk is not repeating them.

What a road-closure alert does

Staff write the notice and publish it. A push lands on the lock screen of everyone who installed the app. The same post sits in the news feed. No Facebook boost, no waiting on a social algorithm.

What staff tap

One dashboard. A post, an event, a directory listing. Whoever currently updates the website can run this. If you take the town bundle, the same post appears on the municipal website without a second step.

Price

$75/mo for the mobile app. $250/mo for the app plus one website. Billed annually, first month free, no implementation fee, no per-resident fee.
