What staff actually do

Since the information on your mobile apps and website is automatically routed to devices, there's no intervention necessary by municipal staff. However, you get all the control in the world through our administrative dashboard. Whether it be a road closure alert, a special town-wide event, or a new business directory listing, your municipality has the ultimate last say to post, edit, or delete any content.

We keep the app — and the site, if you have one — running securely on Canadian servers for your peace of mind. Your town or city gets the last say over the content.

Frequently Asked Questions:

Can we just get the app?

Yes. The iOS and Android app is $150 per month — news, events, school updates, a searchable business directory, and an AI assistant trained with verified town information. We run it and update the content. Staff do not have to intervene; they can take the last say in the administrative dashboard at any time. A public website is not included.

Can we just get a website?

No. The website comes with the app in the $250 plan. The app alone is $150.

What does the $250/mo plan include?

The same customized mobile app plus one public municipal website. That site can be your main town website, a tourism site, or a BIA site. We create, deploy, and maintain it. Content is routed to devices automatically. Your municipality has the last say in the administrative dashboard; we fill in the gaps.

What if we need more than one app or website?

We build as many as you need — a second app, a tourism or BIA site, or a city-scale build. Extra work is quoted. Tell us what you have in mind; depending on complexity we can book a call or send mockups as soon as today. The $150 and $250 plans stay the published rates.

Do our staff have to run this?

No. Information is routed to devices automatically, so no intervention is necessary by municipal staff. They still have the last say through the administrative dashboard — post, edit, or delete any content at any time. You do not need an IT department. We keep the app and site running on Canadian servers.
