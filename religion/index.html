Frequently asked questions

Is every app the same?

No. We custom-build each app tailored to your specific community's needs. We don't lazily reuse designs or systems for different religions.

Do you build the website too?

Yes. Starting at $150/mo, the complete platform includes a matching website to pair with your app.

Can we accept donations?

Yes. One-time and recurring giving, campaign pages with goals, and whichever categories fit your community, from zakat and seva to tithes and a building fund. Donations settle into your own account. Donors get a receipt after each gift. Your institution issues it, with the charity name, amount, and date — and a registration number where that applies for CRA or IRS records.
