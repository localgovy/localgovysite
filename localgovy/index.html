Frequently asked questions

What is a local government mobile app?

A local government mobile app puts your town's news, events, polls, business directory, school updates, and a 24/7 assistant on residents' phones — a direct channel without relying on social media algorithms.

Do you build mobile apps for towns?

Yes. We build branded mobile apps for towns, cities, townships, regional municipalities, counties, and districts across Canada — with your real departments, services, and content.

How long to launch?

You'll see a branded demo within 48 hours of booking. Typical full launch is about 2 weeks, including branding, setup, training, and app-store submission.

Do we need technical staff?

No. Whoever currently updates your website can run the app. Posting news or an alert takes about as long as writing the notice itself, and we train your staff before launch.

How much does a municipality app cost?

The complete platform starts at $150/month, billed annually with the first month free. There's no implementation fee and no per-resident charge, which is where most municipal platforms get expensive.

Can it be customized to our town?

Yes — we implement your municipality's actual departments, services, and content, not just your name, logo, and colours. Every Canadian town, city, township, or regional municipality gets its own distinct app.

Ready to connect your community?

Send us your municipality's name and we'll build a demo around your town within 48 hours. Bring it to council before you commit to anything.
