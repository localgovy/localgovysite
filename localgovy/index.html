What staff actually do

Whoever currently updates your website can run this. A road closure is a post: write it, publish, done. It shows on the website and, if you have the app, on every resident's phone. We train your staff before launch. There is no IT project and no resident database to migrate.

Questions a clerk usually asks

Can we buy only the mobile app?

Yes. The iOS and Android app is $150 per month, including store listings, push notifications, and the resident assistant.

Can we buy only the website?

No. The website comes with the app in the $250 plan. The app alone is $150.

What is the $250 plan?

The app plus one public website. Staff update one dashboard; the same post appears on the site and in the app. That site is usually the town website. It can be a tourism site or a BIA site instead.

What if we need more than one website?

That's quoted. Book a demo, tell us the extra properties — a second app, tourism plus the town site, a BIA site on top of the town site — and we'll send a number. The $150 and $250 plans are the published rates.

Do we need an IT department?

No. Whoever currently updates your website can run this. Posting a road closure takes about as long as writing the notice. We train your staff before launch.

What happens when we book a demo?

Within 48 hours we send a working sample in your town's name and colours. Look at it, show council, then decide. You pay nothing until you are live. Typical launch is about two weeks.

Is this a live town?

No. The screens on these pages are sample builds. We load your real departments, services, and content before anything goes to residents.

Bring a demo to council

Send the municipality's name. Within 48 hours you'll have a working sample in your colours. Show it before you commit to a line item.
