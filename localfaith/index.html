Frequently asked questions

Is every app the same?

No. A mosque app shouldn't feel like a church app. We set up your prayer or service times, language, giving categories, and events rather than handing you a rebranded copy of another community's app.

Is this a white-label template?

No. We build on a proven platform, but every community's app runs on its own real data and workflows — satisfying Apple's and Google's app store rules against duplicate, cosmetic-only apps.

Do you build the website too?

Yes. The $150 Complete Platform includes a matching website connected to the same system as the app. That is different from municipalities, where the app is $150 and a website is added in the $250 plan.

How much does a faith community app cost?

The Complete Platform is $150/month, billed annually with the first month free. It includes a branded app and a matching website, with no setup fee and nothing charged until you're live.

Can we accept donations?

Yes. One-time and recurring giving, campaign pages with goals, and whichever categories fit your community, from zakat and seva to tithes and a building fund. Donations settle into your own account.
